StuyTown

1940 1960 1980 2000 2026

Today, based on personal observation taken over the course of two weeks, it seems that StuyTown is still an area where families live, but there is more young adults to single middle age residents now. Continuing from the 2000's, there seems to be more of a working class mixed with students and young families; while there are some elderly people, the majority is on the younger end. From my observations, the racial make-up of StuyTown still leans majority White, but I have observed more of a mix in ethnicities, seeing many Asians, a few Hispanics or Latinos, but not very many Black people. After comparing to outside data, I reaffirmed my observations, as White residents make-up ~66%, Asians 16%, Hispanic/Latino 9%, Black 3%, and Other ethnicities 6%. There has been an obvious increase in diversity since its start in the 1940's, but it is still far off from being racially inclusive.
