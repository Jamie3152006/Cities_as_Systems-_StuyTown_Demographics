StuyTown

1940 1960 1980 2000 2026

By the '60s, it became a densely populated area for post-war middle class families, being known for its high concentration of young families, with massive amounts of children. The complex was still overwhelmingly White during this time, however, civil rights protests and legal pressures forced the MetLife to allow in a small number of residents that weren't White. In 1960, there were only Black residents, and 16 Puerto Rican residents , the rest were White at 99.7%. Until 1968, where the Fair Housing Act legally banned housing discrimination, StuyTown would continue to be fairly segregated.
